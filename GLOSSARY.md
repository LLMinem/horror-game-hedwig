# Hedwig

Shared vocabulary for the cemetery environment and the game's characters. These definitions describe concepts, not implementation status.

## Language

**Site**:
The actual Neuer Friedhof in Bruchköbel, Germany.
_Avoid_: Map when referring to the real place.

**Cemetery environment**:
The digital, walkable representation of the site, which forms the game's setting.
_Avoid_: Map or setting alone when the distinction from the real site matters.

**Source map**:
The geographic representation of recorded cemetery features used as a reconstruction baseline. It is neither a complete description of the site nor a survey.
_Avoid_: Map when it could mean the cemetery environment.

**Landmark**:
A distinctive building or object that contributes substantially to the site's recognizable identity.

**Representative grave field**:
A grave field that conveys the site's typical arrangement and variety without reproducing every real grave individually.

**Atlas**:
The combined view of spatial sources and linked photographs used for orientation and comparison. It is not an approved construction plan.
_Avoid_: Reference atlas as a separate concept.

**Chapel**:
The chapel near the middle of the site, including its attached extension as one building.
_Avoid_: Funeral hall or hall as a different building unless a specific part is intended.

**Water tap**:
One of the site's water refill points, called a Brunnen in German.
_Avoid_: Fountain when it would imply a decorative water feature.

**Uncle**:
The NPC with dementia who wanders in search of his deceased wife, Hedwig. He is an enemy, but less dangerous than Hedwig.
_Avoid_: Husband as the primary name; it refers to the same character through his relationship to Hedwig.

**Hedwig**:
The ghost of the Uncle's deceased wife and the game's main, most dangerous enemy.
_Avoid_: Wife or Lady as primary character names; use Hedwig to distinguish her from generic roles.
